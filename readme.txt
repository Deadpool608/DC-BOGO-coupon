== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/dc-bogo-coupons` directory, or install the plugin through the WordPress plugins screen directly.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress.
3. Navigate to **Marketing > BOGO Coupons** to create your first offer!

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I create a BOGO coupon in WooCommerce? =
Navigate to **Marketing > BOGO Coupons** in your WordPress dashboard, click **Add New Rule**, configure your "Buy" condition (choose specific products or categories) and your "Get" reward (set discount percentage or 100% free gift), and hit **Publish**.

= Can I create a free gift coupon or gift with purchase offer? =
Yes! DC BOGO Coupons lets you set up automatic free gifts when a customer buys a qualifying product or category, or require a coupon code at checkout for special gift-with-purchase campaigns.

= Does this work with variable products? =
Yes! You can select specific variations or the parent product for both buy rules and free gift rewards.

= Can I limit the offer to specific user roles? =
Yes, you can restrict offers to specific user roles like 'Customer', 'Subscriber', or custom roles.

= Does it support coupon codes? =
Yes, you can choose to apply the BOGO offer automatically or require a WooCommerce coupon code.

= Are category-based BOGO rules really free? =
Yes! Category-based BOGO rules and category discounts are completely free and included out of the box in the DC BOGO coupon plugin.
